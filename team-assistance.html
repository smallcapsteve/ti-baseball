For Teams & Head Coaches

Team Assistance.

Coach Crosby joins your team's practices to run specialized skill work — or steps in to lead a full session when your coaching staff can't be there. Bring pro-level instruction to your team without the travel.

What Coach Can Do At Your Practice

Pick What Your Team Needs.

Hitting Instruction

Swing mechanics, bat speed, contact drills, approach, timing, situational hitting. From basic fundamentals to advanced pitch-recognition work.

Catching Development

Receiving, framing, footwork, blocking, throwing to bases, pitch calling. Positional instruction from someone who caught at the pro level.

Pitching Support

Mechanics, arm care, pitch design, command work, and bullpen sessions with video feedback. Age-appropriate volume + intensity.

Fielding & Defense

Infield and outfield fundamentals, footwork, throwing accuracy, cut-offs and relays, game situations and communication.

Cover for Absent Coaches

Head coach out of town? Assistant unavailable? Coach can step in to run a full team practice so your athletes stay on schedule.

Position-Specific Clinics

One-day clinics focused on a single skill for your whole team — e.g., a 90-minute catching clinic for your two catchers plus their backups.

How It Works

Simple Booking.

  • Send the details below — your team name, age group, what you'd like Coach to focus on, and a few dates that could work.
  • Coach will reply within 24 hours with availability and pricing tailored to your ask (one-off session, multi-week program, or clinic format).
  • Coach travels to your practice location within the GTA. Longer distances by arrangement.
  • Bring what you have — Coach adapts to whatever gear and space you're working with.
