Winter Development Program

Winter Development.

Three streams. Same structured, progressive baseball development. Small groups, focused instruction, and every session covers hitting, defense, and strength & conditioning. Season starts October 31.

3 Streams

Why Winter Development

Structured. Progressive. Age-Appropriate.

Every stream runs the same structured curriculum — 1/3 hitting, 1/3 defense/pitching/catching, 1/3 strength & conditioning — but grouped by age so instruction matches ability. Season kicks off October 31, with 8-week phases you can commit to individually or bundle for the full season.

The Catchers-only stream is a specialized track for athletes who want to focus on receiving, framing, throwing, blocking, and pitch calling.

  • Same curriculum across all three streams — hitting, defense/throwing/catching, strength & conditioning.
  • Small group format for quality reps and personalized coaching.
  • Choose your commitment — 8, 16, or 24 weeks — or try a $75 drop-in.
  • Season starts October 31, 2026 at B360 in Ajax.
