Development Program · Ages 8–12

TI Baseball Academy.

A structured, progressive baseball development program designed to build complete players. Saturdays 1:00–3:00 PM at B360, starting October 31. Minimum 8 players per session.

Saturdays

Why The Academy

Complete Players. Every Saturday.

The Academy is our answer for ages 8–12 who want structured, progressive baseball development in a real training environment. Every Saturday afternoon, players work through all three phases of the game — hitting, defense, and athletic performance — in a high-energy setting built to grow skill, confidence, and game IQ.

Small groups (minimum 8 players per session) keep instruction focused. Each phase runs 8 weeks, and families can commit to just one phase, mix and match two, or run the full 24-week season for the deepest development track.

  • Ages 8–12, structured for skill progression across the season.
  • Saturdays, 1:00–3:00 PM at B360 — indoor turf, cages, and pitching lanes.
  • Every session covers all three phases — hitting, defense/throwing/catching, and strength & conditioning.
  • Small group format — quality instruction with real reps.

Every Session

Three Blocks. Every Saturday.

1/3 Hitting

Swing development · bat speed · contact · approach.

1/3 Fielding, Pitching, Catching

Defensive fundamentals · throwing mechanics · position-specific development.

1/3 Strength & Conditioning

Athletic development · movement · strength · speed.
